## 1. Summary
Rainbow Pour: Water Sort ("the app") is a puzzle game for people aged 13 and over. We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

## 2. Information stored on your device
Game progress, gems, settings, the nickname and avatar you choose, and daily reward data are stored only on your device. We do not send them to our servers. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

## 3. Information collected by third-party services
- **Google AdMob** shows ads. It may collect your Advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent first using Google's consent form (UMP). You can change your choice in Settings > Ad privacy options. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Firebase Analytics** collects anonymous usage events (for example level started/completed) and **Firebase Crashlytics** collects crash reports and basic device information, to improve the game. These do not include your nickname.

## 4. Ads and rewards
The app contains ads. Some ads are optional rewarded ads; watching one gives in-game gems. Gems have no cash value.

## 5. Children
The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## 6. Data sharing and security
We do not sell personal information. Data processed by Google and Firebase is transmitted over encrypted connections.

## 7. Your choices
You can reset your advertising ID in your Android settings, change ad consent in the app, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no account data, there is nothing to delete on our side. For questions, write to [contact email].

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
